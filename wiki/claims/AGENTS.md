# Claims domain rules

Extends the platform AGENTS.md. Applies to everything under claims/.

- The core questions per claim: what happened and when (timeline.md), does
  cover respond (coverage.md), what is reserved and why (decisions.md), and
  what remains unresolved (open-questions.md).
- coverage.md records competing interpretations side by side. A coverage
  position is only stated as settled once a decision record exists.
- Always link back to the originating submission: wording analysis starts
  from what was bound, not from the claim narrative.
- Reserve movements above EUR 500K require a decision record before entry.
