# Lessons

## L-001 - Vendor standing access is a distinct exposure path
The bound submission modelled ransomware and outage but treated the
vendor-operated WMS as a pricing note, not an exposure path. The claim
entered entirely through vendor infrastructure. Candidate for platform
promotion: appetite and wording review for vendor-operated systems in
cyber-logistics - explicit questions at submission (who operates, what
standing access exists, whose controls are scheduled) and wording that
allocates vendor risk explicitly instead of leaving CY-EX-04 ambiguous.
References: [[submissions/SUB-2024-018/index|SUB-2024-018]], coverage.md this case, forensics-report source
note.

## S-001 - Skill candidate - vendor-access review checklist
Proposed by: claims agent (session 2024-11-26). A repeatable checklist for
assessing vendor access on any cyber-logistics case. Awaiting human review
for promotion to platform-ic/skills/.
