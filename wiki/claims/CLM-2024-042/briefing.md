# Briefing - at close

Attackers compromised the WMS vendor and used its standing remote-access
channel into NovaFreight's warehouse management system. Ransomware
encrypted WMS servers on 2024-06-09; all 14 sites lost dispatch capability
for 9 days. Offline backups held - restoration, not ransom, ended the
outage.

Business-interruption loss EUR 1.7M, incident-response EUR 0.4M. Coverage
was disputed under CY-EX-04 and resolved for the insured (see coverage.md).
Settled EUR 2.1M on 2024-11-21.

Assessment: the loss itself was well within modelled ransomware scenarios.
What was not modelled was the entry path - vendor standing access. That is
the lesson this case leaves behind.
