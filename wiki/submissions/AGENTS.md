# Underwriting domain rules

Extends the platform AGENTS.md. Applies to everything under submissions/.

- The core questions per submission: does the risk fit appetite, what
  exclusions apply, is the exposure priced, and who has authority to bind.
- Appetite positions live in coverage-or-appetite.md and cite the appetite
  entity in the knowledge graph, not a document quote.
- Always check claims history on the same insured AND the same risk class:
  run a precedent query before drafting any appetite position.
- Authority: assessments above EUR 5M aggregate limit require a senior
  underwriter decision recorded in decisions.md before any bind step.
