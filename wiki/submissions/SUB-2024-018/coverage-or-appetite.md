# Coverage and appetite

- Fits cyber-logistics appetite: mid-market, EU, no US exposure.
- Cover: business-interruption, incident-response, data-restoration.
- Exclusions applied: systems-integrity exclusion CY-EX-04 (loss caused by
  failure to maintain agreed security controls), war and state-actor
  exclusion CY-EX-01.
- Not covered: cyber-extortion payments (declined by insured to reduce
  premium).

Assessment: CY-EX-04 wording assumes the insured controls the systems in
scope. Joint vendor operation of the WMS was not examined against this
assumption.
