# Briefing - at bind

NovaFreight runs 14 warehouses across BeNeLux and northern Germany on a
single warehouse management system. Revenue EUR 180M, thin IT team, WMS
operated jointly with an external vendor. MFA on staff accounts, offline
backups tested quarterly.

Assessment at the time: standard mid-market logistics cyber risk, ransomware
the driver, network-outage secondary. Vendor topology noted but not treated
as a distinct exposure path.

Bound at EUR 4M aggregate with a 15% pricing load for WMS concentration.
