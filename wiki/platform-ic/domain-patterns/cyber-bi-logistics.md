# Business interruption in logistics cyber

Logistics BI losses concentrate on dispatch capability, not data. A WMS
outage halts revenue across every dependent site simultaneously - single
points of failure multiply exposure across locations. Pricing should load
for system concentration (sites per system), and restoration time drives
loss more than ransom economics where offline backups are tested.
