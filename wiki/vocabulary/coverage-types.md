# Coverage types - controlled vocabulary

| term                   | definition                                           |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| business-interruption  | Loss of income from covered system unavailability    |
| incident-response      | Forensics, containment, and recovery costs           |
| cyber-extortion        | Ransom payments and negotiation costs where lawful   |
| data-restoration       | Cost of restoring or recreating lost data            |
| liability-third-party  | Claims by others arising from the insured's breach   |
