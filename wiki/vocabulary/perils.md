# Perils - controlled vocabulary

| term                  | definition                                            |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| ransomware            | Malicious encryption of systems with extortion demand |
| data-breach           | Unauthorised access to or exfiltration of data        |
| vendor-compromise     | Intrusion via a third-party supplier's access         |
| network-outage        | Loss of availability without malicious encryption     |
| social-engineering    | Fraud through impersonation of trusted parties        |
