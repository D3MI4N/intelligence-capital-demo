# First notification of loss - NovaFreight Logistics BV

Received via placing broker, 2024-06-09 16:40 CET.

At approximately 03:00 CET the insured's warehouse management system became
unavailable at all sites. On-screen messages at the Rotterdam operations
centre indicated file encryption and demanded contact through a payment
portal. The insured's IT provider confirmed ransomware affecting the WMS
production estate.

Dispatch is halted at all 14 warehouses. Receiving continues on paper at
reduced volume. The insured has activated its incident plan, isolated the
WMS network segment, and engaged its retained forensics firm.

A ransom demand of USD 1.2M in cryptocurrency was received. The insured
states its intention to restore from offline backups rather than engage
with the demand, subject to confirmation that backups are unaffected.

Initial loss indication: outage of 7 to 10 days across all sites.
Contractual penalty clauses with two major retail customers activate after
48 hours of missed dispatch windows.

Policy reference: cyber policy bound February 2024 (submission
SUB-2024-018). Notification within policy terms.
