# Loss adjuster final report - CLM-2024-042

Final report, 2024-11-18. Supersedes interim reports of July and
September 2024.

## Business interruption
Dispatch was halted at all 14 sites from 2024-06-09 to 2024-06-18, nine
calendar days. Adjusted BI loss is EUR 1.70M, comprising lost margin on
undelivered volumes, contractual penalties to two retail customers under
48-hour dispatch clauses, and incremental costs of partial manual
operation. Mitigation credit has been applied for volumes recovered in the
two weeks following restoration.

## Incident response and restoration
Forensics, containment, emergency IT labour and restoration costs are
adjusted at EUR 0.40M against invoices reviewed.

## Recommendation
Total adjusted loss EUR 2.10M against a EUR 4M aggregate limit.
Settlement at EUR 2.10M is recommended. The coverage position adopted on
2024-09-15 (exclusion CY-EX-04 not applicable) is reflected; no policy
sub-limits are engaged.
